# Daily Word design

The `logo:`, `look:` and `accent:` lines are recorded only through the design skill's three questions, which are Ashley's to answer. Until then Daily Word keeps the Futures brand look it already has (`--dw-*` tokens, white page).

## Screens and their one next step

`route · who it is for · the ONE main button, as a verb and an object`

- `/staff` → Settings → Campuses, a row open · the owner (admin staff) · Save campus. Works out for them: the campus's saved values filled in; under Town, "Other towns near this campus" (one per line) is how new readers' towns find the campus, so a campus added in a town is guessed from its own Town with no developer, and a town written under two campuses of the same kind (the Adelaide metro) gives readers there a short list instead of one guess.
- `/staff` → Settings → Campuses, nothing open · the owner (admin staff) · Add a campus
- More → Sign in as pastor, signed in → Change password open · a pastor or staff member signed in on this phone · Change password. Worked out for them: who is signed in and that this phone stays signed in; pressing early names the one box to fill, beside the button, and puts the cursor in it
- `/staff job form` · campus, hub and media staff · Put this on the campus corner / Put this on the congregation page (by job, as today). Works out for them: the campus (locked for a campus pastor), the congregation they picked last time, and which questions they may reword (the server sends `rewordable`).
- `/staff job form`, wording editor open · admin, and hub on hub forms · Save wording. Works out for them: the question's current words are already in the boxes; only the person's new words are asked for; their typed answer is kept.
- `/staff job form`, failed to load · campus, hub and media staff · Load the form again. Works out for them: why the form is empty (it did not load), said where the button would be, with the one way out.
- `/` (Campus tab, no campus yet) · a reader · Yes (confirm the guessed campus). The app works out the campus first (the link or QR code she came by, then her Planning Center record, looked up from the email she signed in with and never asked, then her town, matched against each campus's Town and the other towns the owner lists under it, then her region) and asks one question with the guess filled in: "Are you part of Futures Kennesaw?" Yes · Another campus. Another campus leads with her region's campuses, then "Somewhere else". With no single guess: "Which Futures campus are you part of?" with her region first. Nothing is saved until she taps.
- `/more` (Settings) · a reader · none: the sentence, then today's rows. The app works out one sentence from what she already set ("You read ESV, one chapter a day, with a reminder at 7:00 am. Your campus is Futures Kennesaw."), so she reads her set-up instead of scanning rows; every row stays below it.
- `/` Home · every reader (comfort, I'm New, congregation, pastor and study paths) · the next step as a verb and an object: Read {passage} / Read Day {n} / Open today's sermon notes / Write it down; when people prayed for a request she posted from this phone, "{n} people prayed for your request this week." See your request (after the reading, before Write it down); when nothing is left, no button, only "Tomorrow: {passage}". The app works out which one from the reading, the journey day, the reflection, the campus's Sunday clock and the published notes, so the reader never has to look for it; everything else waits one tap away under More for today. Listen Now (the round play button on the photo) is the hero's own control, not a second main button; the chapter slider under it only moves through the book, so it carries `data-mo-allow="scrubber"`. Also worked out for them: today's chapters, where they left off, which chapter is playing.
- `/` Campus tab → Prayer Wall · a reader · Add prayer. - `/staff` home, hub role (QuickNotes card) · hub and admin staff · Put this on the congregation page (after the preview; before it, See the notes page).
