## reference_daily_word_sw_books_cache_trap

**Trap (found 1 Sep 2026):** `public/sw.js` in ~/futures-daily-word served `/bible/*` AND `/books/*` cache-first out of `fdw-bible-v1`. That cache is deliberately preserved by the SW `activate()` purge and by the `index.html` one-time reset (it holds the offline KJV), and the pathway fetch URL carries no version. So an edit to `books/faith-pathway.json` (or any book JSON) was pinned at whatever a device first downloaded — forever. Symptom: the 40-day journey's Reflect & Respond questions (added 20:49 ET) were live in the prod chunk yet invisible on every device that had opened the journey earlier, including Ashley's.

**Fix — [DW PR #84](https://github.com/AEGLOBALUSA/futures-daily-word/pull/84)** (branch `fix/pathway-json-stale-cache`, worktree ~/futures-daily-word-questions): `/books/*` network-first with cached copy as offline fallback only; `/bible/*` stays cache-first; caches v50→v51, SW_VERSION v65→v66; netlify.toml `/books/*` max-age 86400→0 + must-revalidate (ETag ⇒ 304). Returning readers get fresh content on the SECOND open after deploy (first open installs the SW and reloads).

**How to apply:**
- Prod bundle grep for a string is NOT proof a returning user sees it — check the SW strategy for the asset class first. Three version knobs exist: `CACHE_NAME`/`STATIC_CACHE` in sw.js, `SW_VERSION` in main.tsx, `V` in index.html's one-time reset; bump the first two together for any sw.js change.
- The app's screens are lazy chunks (`HomeScreen-*.js`); `index-*.js` is only the entry. Grep the right chunk.
- Harness for sw.js behaviour lives in the session scratchpad pattern: vm-load sw.js with stubbed `caches`/`fetch`, dispatch fake fetch events.
